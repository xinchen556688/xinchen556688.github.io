Museum Label Writing: Formats, Lengths and Common Mistakes

A wall label, an alt text attribute and a caption are often treated as three names for the same thing. They are not. Each serves a different reader, obeys different length limits, and fails in a different way when it is done badly.

Below is a practical breakdown of all three formats, including what belongs in each, what to leave out, and where the overlap actually helps rather than creating duplication.
